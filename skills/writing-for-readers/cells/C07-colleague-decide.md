# C7. Colleague, decide

One cell of the [writing taxonomy](../taxonomy.md): classify there first, then read only this file. Every cell has the same shape, and the why sits beside each rule.

**Artefacts:** recommendations, options papers, decision forms and review pages, questionnaires to a colleague.
**Reader does:** chooses.
**Reviewed:** yes: the reader is the reviewer.

| Property | Setting |
|---|---|
| Speculation | labelled, and each option says what will actually happen if it is chosen |
| History | only what changes the choice |
| Length | the ask first, then a two-sentence summary, then the options; under a page |
| Tense | present; imperative in the ask |
| Markers | mandatory on any claim an option rests on |
| Vocabulary | internal |
| Internals | only where they decide between options |
| Formatting | numbered questions; two to four options each; the recommendation marked on the option; a free-text field always visible |

**Must contain**
- The question, one idea, because a compound question comes back half-answered.
- The claim, the rule and the conflict in the question's own text, because a question written in back-references to something the reader must look up comes back unanswerable.
- Options that say what happens, never a bare yes or no where the real choice has three outcomes.
- A recommendation, because a diagnosis with no recommendation hands over the expensive half of the work.

**Must not contain**
- "See above", "as discussed", "per question 3".
- More than one idea per question.
- An option you would not actually do.

**Tells**
- Exact: a question with fewer than two or more than four options; no recommendation marked; "see above", "as discussed", "per Q".
- Judgement: a compound question; option padding.

**Examples**
- Defect: "Should we upgrade the database? Yes / No"
- Good: "Q1. The database is two major versions behind and stops getting security fixes in March. Options: (a) upgrade in the January maintenance window, with one hour of read-only time (recommended); (b) upgrade in March, against the deadline; (c) move to the managed service instead, about three weeks of work."
