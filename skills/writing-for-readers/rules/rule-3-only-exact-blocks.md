# Rule 3. Only exact rules may block

One of the three rules the [writing taxonomy](../taxonomy.md) absorbed. The first line of the body names the cells it governs.

Governs the review contract. Never block on a heuristic that can be wrong. A gate that over-blocks gets routed around within a week and then protects nothing, so over-blocking is not the safe direction of error. A hard rule that rests on a judgement call is split, not forced: whether a citation *exists* is exact and may block, whether it is *adequate* is judgement and may only flag. That is why every cell's tells are split into exact and judgement, and why a standalone review never blocks at all.
